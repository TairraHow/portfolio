CI Green Belt — Sourcing Intake Waste Elimination
Continuous Improvement · Process Design · Change Management · Stakeholder Adoption
40% Error Reduction · Sustained
Situation

03
Inventory Accuracy Restoration — 77% to 99%
Root Cause Analysis · SAP Systems · Data Integrity · Cross-Functional Resolution
99% Accuracy Restored · Working Capital Impact
Situation
An RDC was reporting inventory accuracy at 77% — creating order cancellations, poor customer experience, and loss of confidence in SAP data integrity. The root cause was not visible to the team and the impact on working capital reporting was significant.
Action
Diagnosed a systemic Goods Issue process failure that had been suppressing accuracy without detection. Coordinated cross-functional resolution across operations, IT, and logistics — correcting SAP records, implementing proper write-off documentation, and establishing daily cycle count processes to sustain accuracy.
Result
Inventory accuracy restored to 99%. Order cancellations reduced. SAP data reliability re-established across the site. Direct positive impact on working capital reporting accuracy — a root cause others had missed for months.
04
DFOA Transportation Zone Standardization (SAP)
SAP Configuration · Process Standardization · Financial Governance · Canada Network
$100K+ Loss Prevented · Rate Disputes Eliminated
Situation
Canada's DFOA freight rates were inconsistent — no standard method for classifying customers by zone was creating rate disputes, incorrect payments exceeding $100K, and ongoing customer experience issues across the Canadian distribution network.
Action
Designed a standardized zoning model using FSA codes. Aligned rates across three zones per province. Embedded the solution directly into SAP. Created governance documentation (QRG) and trained Customer Operations on the new process to ensure sustained adoption.
Result
$100K+ in losses prevented. Rate disputes eliminated. Repeatable, governed process embedded in SAP. Improved customer experience across Canada. A problem that had persisted for months resolved with a structured, system-level solution.

Financial Governance · Process Design · Accountability Infrastructure · Claims Management
