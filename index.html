06
DFOA Transportation Zone Standardization (SAP)
SAP Configuration · Data Governance · Process Standardization · Canada Network
$100K+ CAD Prevented · Rate Disputes Eliminated
Situation
Canada's DFOA freight rates were inconsistent — no standard method for classifying customers by zone was creating rate disputes, incorrect payments exceeding $100K CAD, and customer experience issues across the Canadian distribution network.
Action
Designed a standardized zoning model using FSA codes. Aligned rates across three zones per province. Embedded the solution in SAP configuration. Created governance documentation (QRG) and trained Customer Operations for sustained adoption.
Result
$100K+ CAD prevented. Rate disputes eliminated. Governed process embedded in SAP. Improved customer experience across Canada. A configuration problem persisting for months resolved with a system-level solution.
